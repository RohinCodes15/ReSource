# Demo video script

Target length **2 minutes 50 seconds**. The CA-17 page asks for 1 to 3 minutes and says videos outside that range may be penalized, so do not run over. It also lists what the video must include; each one is marked **(required)** below.

Record against the deployed site if it is up, or against your own computer (`README.md`, "Run it"). Use a phone-sized browser window, 390 pixels wide. Set up before recording: language English, location cleared, storage banner already answered, and one report already filed and verified in Admin so Community Impact is not empty.

Say it in your own words. The lines are a guide to content and timing, not a text to read out.

## If you are short on time

Cut the map (1:15 to 1:35 becomes Resources only) before cutting anything marked required.

## Things not to say

- Do not say the data updates on its own. It is entered by hand from public documents.
- Do not say the City supports, uses or has reviewed the app.
- Do not give a number of users, or say residents rely on it. It has not launched.
- Do not say how any council member voted.
- Do not say the translations are checked. They have not been reviewed by native speakers.
- Do not describe AI-written work as your own. Say what you directed, decided and understand.
