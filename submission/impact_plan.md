# Impact plan

How ReSource could reach the people it is for. Nothing here has happened yet: no organization has been contacted, no partnership exists, and no resident has used the app. This is a plan.

## Goal for the first eight weeks

1. Ten residents use the app while someone watches, and their feedback changes it.
2. Two local organizations agree to check their own listing and tell their clients about it.
3. A native speaker reviews each of the Dari, Chinese, Vietnamese and Spanish translations.
4. One council office or City department sees the Community Impact page and says whether verified reports in that form are useful to them.

Measure only what can be counted honestly: sessions watched, corrections received, organizations that replied, reviews completed. The app has no analytics, so it cannot count visitors, and that is by design.

## Feedback survey

Keep it under five minutes. Ask in person where possible, and ask in the person's language. Do not collect names.

**About you (optional)**
1. Which part of Fremont do you live in? (ZIP code area)
2. Which language do you prefer to read in?

**Using the app** (ask them to try "What changed for me?" first, without help)
3. What did you expect this app to do when you first opened it?
4. Did you find out anything about a city service that you did not know? What?
5. Was anything confusing? Where did you get stuck?
6. Did you tap a source link? Did it help you trust the information?
7. Is there a service you use that you looked for and could not find?
8. If you read it in a language other than English: did anything sound wrong or unnatural? Which words?

**Usefulness**
9. Have the budget changes affected you or your family? How?
10. Did the app point you to somewhere that could help with that?
11. Would you report an impact through this app? Why or why not?
12. Who do you know who should see this?

**Trust**
13. Who did you think made this app before you were told?
14. Is there anything that would make you trust it more, or less?

**One number**
15. How likely are you to open this again, from 1 (never) to 5 (definitely)?

## After the first eight weeks

- Fix what the sessions showed, most-mentioned first.
- Re-check every source and update the dates (`docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`).
- Ask the City whether it can publish service changes as a simple table, so the app does not depend on one person reading PDFs.
- Decide, with an adult, whether the project continues, hands over to someone, or ends. If it ends, the privacy policy says all resident data is deleted.
