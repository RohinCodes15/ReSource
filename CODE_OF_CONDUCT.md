# Code of conduct

ReSource is a community project about a subject people feel strongly about: their city's services and how public money is spent. This page applies to everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are students or first-time contributors.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Stick to what can be shown. In a project about public facts, say where a claim comes from.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information, including a resident's report or contact details.
- Keep partisan campaigning out of it. The project describes what public documents say; it does not take sides on candidates or ballot measures.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's age, race, ethnicity, national origin, language, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or immigration status.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately adding false information to the data.
- Continuing after being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Email rohincodes15@gmail.com. Say what happened and where, with links if you have them. Reports are kept private. If the report concerns the project's maintainer, a participant under 18, or anything that may be unsafe, tell a trusted adult such as a parent or teacher as well.

## What happens next

The maintainer will read every report and respond as soon as he can. Depending on what happened, the response may be a private note, a public reminder, removal of a comment or contribution, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

## Scope

This applies in the project's repository and anywhere someone is representing the project. It does not replace your school's rules or the law.
